STEP 1 · VISUAL EXPLANATION

What is a recursion base case?

A recursive function needs a condition that tells it when to stop. Without it, the function keeps calling itself.

factorial(3)
↓
3 × factorial(2)
↓
2 × factorial(1)
↓
factorial(0) → 1 BASE CASE — STOP HERE
